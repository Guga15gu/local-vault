Local Vault

Local Vault is a personal, local-first media library. It keeps videos and PDFs on the owner's own devices and can sync files created by the app itself with the owner's own Google Drive.

  • No public server, no accounts, no sign-ups.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Google Drive access is limited to the drive.file scope: the app can only see and change files it created.
